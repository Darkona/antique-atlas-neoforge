# Antique Atlas

A hand-drawn world map that fills itself in as you explore. Press **M** to open it.

![The atlas](images/atlas-map.png)

Antique Atlas is a client-side mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1** on **NeoForge 21.1**. It draws the map that [Surveyor Map Framework](https://github.com/Darkona/surveyor-neoforge/wiki) records, so you need both mods.
